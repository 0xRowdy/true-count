# Blackjack training modes and strategy evidence

Research date: 2026-09-05. Resolves [Blackjack training modes and strategy evidence](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/11); informs [Launch game rules and practice strategies](https://github.com/0xRowdy/true-count/issues/3). All launch selections below are proposals, not approved product decisions.

## Findings

The four requested combinations are coherent if the app separates **playing policy**, **count responsibility**, and **betting policy**. Count-index deviations require count information, but a learner can practice interpreting a supplied count before maintaining one. The mode matrix below is a product proposal inferred from these distinct skills, not a claim that an existing provider offers precisely these four modes.

Blackjack Apprenticeship (BJA) distinguishes playing deviations from changing bets and says the count must be reevaluated at the playing decision, since cards revealed after betting change it. Its suggested learning sequence prioritizes basic strategy, counting, conversion, and betting before adding playing deviations. These are useful first-party teaching recommendations, not controlled evidence that one sequence suits every learner. [BJA deviation explanation](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/blackjack-deviations/)

| Proposed mode | Intended learner and prerequisite | Who supplies count information? | Playing and betting assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Beginner; understand hand totals and available actions | No count required | Rule-specific basic decisions; fixed practice wager initially |
| Basic with deviations | Intermediate; reliable basic decisions and understanding of index thresholds | App supplies count and its convention; optionally supplies running count plus decks remaining for a conversion step | Apply a selected index set; otherwise follow basic strategy. No claim of independent counting proficiency |
| Counting with basic | Intermediate; basic decisions plus card-value/running-count drills | Player maintains running count; app can supply shoe depth before later requiring player estimation | Score count and conversion separately; playing policy stays basic. Optional count-based betting exercise |
| Counting with deviations | Advanced; count/conversion accuracy and selected indices | Player maintains count and, at full difficulty, estimates remaining decks | Integrate count, conversion, index decisions, and optionally count-based betting |

For the second mode, supplying only an integer true count is insufficient for a curriculum with running-count-sign exceptions: supply the relevant running count too. BJA's H17 chart expressly treats `0+` and `0-` as positive and negative **running** counts; its other positive/negative suffixes specify threshold direction. Thus an index record needs count type and comparison semantics, not just an integer. [BJA H17 chart](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/BJA_H17.pdf)

## Deviation coverage and correctness boundaries

The Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 are credible bounded candidates. Michael Shackleford republishes Don Schlesinger's tables with explicit permission and identifies their source editions. His accompanying simulations use specified six-deck rules and show that a compact index set captures much of the benefit in that setting. They do not validate those exact values for every deck count and rule permutation. [Wizard of Odds Hi-Lo analysis and authorized tables](https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/card-counting/high-low/)

Proposed progression: introduce insurance and a few hard-total decisions first; expand to the chosen complete compact set; add surrender indices only where surrender is supported. Preserve pair/total distinctions and legal-action precedence. For example, a hard-total hit/stand deviation must not override an available superior surrender, or silently replace a separate pair decision. This is a proposed coaching requirement; the source's basic-strategy instructions check surrender before splits, doubles, and hit/stand. [BJA decision order](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/blackjack-strategy-charts/)

Do not mix introductory rounding examples into a universal scoring rule. BJA displays −7 ÷ 2 as −3: this is consistent with truncation toward zero for that example and inconsistent with flooring to −4, but does not establish its complete rounding specification. Shackleford illustrates 7 ÷ 4 = 1.75 and rounding to 2, inconsistent with truncating that positive example. These are different inputs, so they are evidence of **incomplete, potentially incompatible teaching conventions**, not proof the providers disagree on every input. Specify integer conversion, tie handling, deck-estimation resolution, and index-generation assumptions together before implementing a graded curriculum. [BJA conversion example](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/how-to-count-cards/), [Wizard conversion example](https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/card-counting/high-low/)

Basic-rule corrections also differ from count deviations. BJA explicitly describes its general chart as a simplified choice across deck counts, whereas Shackleford supplies separate H17/S17 instructions and DAS-dependent split decisions. A generic chart therefore cannot establish exact coaching coverage for every configurable game. [BJA chart philosophy](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/blackjack-strategy-charts/), [Wizard 4–8 deck basic strategy](https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/strategy/4-decks/)

Hand-composition exceptions can exist without tracking previous rounds. Shackleford separately analyzes total-dependent and composition-dependent surrender, including pair-of-eights exceptions. Treat those as a distinct possible advanced curriculum; do not use them to imply that ordinary count-index deviations require no count information. [Original surrender analysis](https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/surrender/)

## Proposed bounded launch

1. Offer the four table modes above, plus the already-agreed card-value recognition, running count, true-count conversion, and counting-during-table-play exercises. BJA confirms separate basic, running-count, conversion, deviation, and betting practice categories; its public page does not establish the exact four-mode product design. [BJA training offering](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/blackjack-training-drills/)
2. Use Hi-Lo only initially. Make the selected playing policy visible so counting-with-basic does not mark an intentional basic decision wrong because an unselected index policy prefers another action.
3. Start the candidate rule matrix at six decks, U.S. hole-card/peek dealing, 3:2 blackjack, doubling any initial two cards, DAS, fixed split/ace restrictions, and named H17/S17 presets with late surrender on/off. This is a **four-combination candidate**, not a verified final catalog. Set all remaining payout, split, insurance, shuffle, and visibility details explicitly. Add eight decks, no-DAS, or additional variants only after complete coaching coverage is demonstrated for each added combination.
4. Every supported combination must have matching basic and selected-deviation coaching. Bound configuration to the verified combinations; do not expose extra supported conditions with approximate generic advice. Single/double deck, European no-hole-card settlement variants, 6:5, early surrender, side bets, and specialty games are proposed exclusions pending their own evidence and product decisions.
5. Specify one named index family and conversion convention. Validate H17/S17 and surrender interactions, zero/sign boundaries, unavailable doubles/splits, and card reveal timing before accepting its complete matrix. Evidence gathered here is sufficient to choose a bounded direction; it is not an exhaustive action-table certification.
6. Keep wagering practice an independent option: fixed wagers isolate playing/counting skills; a documented virtual-unit ramp teaches following count-based bet instructions. Ramp adherence is not proof of profitable or suitably funded casino play. The cited Hi-Lo simulations explicitly vary spread and penetration, so no single profitability estimate applies to an unspecified table. [Wizard simulation conditions](https://wizardofodds.com/games/blackjack/card-counting/high-low/)

Proposed scoring separates basic decisions, deviation decisions, running-count errors, deck-estimation errors, conversion errors, betting adherence, and observed outcomes. In combined practice, preserve both the actual count and the learner's entered count so feedback can distinguish a missed card from an incorrect index application. These are product-design inferences; no pedagogical efficacy study was located or claimed.

## Remaining decisions and source limits

- Confirm whether “basic with deviations” supplies relevant counts; this remains a proposed interpretation of the user's requested mode.
- Select exact named presets, split restrictions, configurable conditions, index source/version, conversion convention, and initial subset/full-set coverage.
- Decide whether betting practice belongs in launch table modes and approve its virtual-unit specification independently.
- BJA is verified as a first-party training inspiration. Its promotional profit/accuracy claims and testimonials are not mathematical guarantees or independently established learning outcomes. QFIT supplies original simulator documentation and simulation results; Shackleford supplies original analyses and expressly authorized index reproductions. Do not describe the proposed catalog as endorsed by any of them.
- Public availability and another site's stated permission to reproduce a chart do not establish True Count's permission to copy that chart. This research links evidence and proposes original teaching structure; content reuse remains a separate release-readiness decision.
