Throwaway visual/navigation prototype · issue #30

Mobile visual direction and core navigation

Three structurally different visual systems and top-level navigations for reaching blackjack, standard craps, roulette, counting challenges, strategy creation/community, and saved records. Flip variants with the floating switcher (bottom of screen), pick a device size, and step through the six representative screens. Nothing here saves data, deals cards, or runs a real session — labels like "session active" and "hidden count" are fixed examples to show the intended behavior, not a working game.

  • Check's running count stays visually hidden in every screen and state — never rendered, even as placeholder digits.
  • Every screen includes a note on what happens to an in-progress session when you navigate away.
