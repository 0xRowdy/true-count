## Question

What source-backed craps practice strategies span beginner, intermediate, and advanced players? Research a broad candidate range, explaining executable behavior, learning value, complexity, required table conditions, and mathematical limitations. Recommend five complementary launch candidates as proposals. Use authoritative original mathematical analysis or official rules and verify any strategy attribution to Casino Quest or CEG Dealer School against their own material. Flag ambiguous strategy names or missing execution details.

## Answer

Researched 13 executable candidate families across beginner, intermediate, and advanced practice in [Craps strategies](../research/craps-strategies.md). Proposed five: Pass with odds; Flat place 6 and 8; Don't pass with lay odds; bounded Three-point Molly; capped press-and-collect 6 and 8. Findings distinguish per-roll and per-resolved-wager house edge, odds dilution versus expected dollar loss, and exact table dependencies. Casino Quest's own store verifies Original and Ultimate Squeeze Play as distinct variants; complete public execution remains unavailable. CEG Triple Lux attribution was not verified from retrievable first-party material and is not assumed. Research is complete; candidate selection and executable defaults are proposals awaiting product decisions.
