## Answer

Evidence investigation complete: [Blackjack training modes and strategy evidence](../research/blackjack-strategies.md).

The requested four modes can separate playing policy, count responsibility, and optional betting practice. Supplying the count for basic-with-deviations is a coherent proposal, still awaiting product confirmation. Hi-Lo is the strongest bounded launch candidate; KO/REKO and more complex systems are documented alternatives, not necessary advanced tiers. A compact, rule-specific deviation set is supported by authoritative teaching and original simulations, but exact indices and conversion conventions must be selected and validated together. BJA's running-count-sign exceptions and incomplete introductory rounding conventions make a generic true-count integer insufficient for every drill.

Recommend bounding configurable conditions to combinations with complete matching coaching. The research proposes a six-deck H17/S17, late-surrender on/off candidate matrix, with other rules fixed explicitly, not an approved catalog or exhaustive coaching certification. Exact presets, supplied-count behavior, index family, rounding, betting practice, and content reuse remain open product/release decisions.
