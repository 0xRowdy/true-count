## Answer

Research completed in [Roulette strategy research](../research/roulette-strategies.md) on 2026-09-05. Twelve candidates span beginner placement, intermediate staking, and advanced bookkeeping/table fluency. Five complementary proposals are flat even-money, fixed two-dozen coverage, capped Martingale, three-win parlay, and bounded Labouchere. Official settlement rules, original mathematical analyses, and academic research support the findings. The report separates complexity from expected return and records zero, bankroll, limit, and naming ambiguities. Research is resolved; exact launch selection and variant/compatibility choices remain open in the parent product issue.
